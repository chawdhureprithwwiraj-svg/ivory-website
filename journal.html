Notes from the house.

Short public entries in the house's own voice: what changed, what is coming, what mood the rooms are in. Never a member's story, never a member's name.

22 OCTOBER 2026

The house learns to sing.

Every touch on the house now carries its own note: a marimba under the menus, a gold chime under the buttons, a paper bell when a question opens, a deep gong at the Vault door. Thirteen voices in all, each synthesised at the moment of your touch and left to fade through a soft hall.

No audio files live here and nothing is tracked. If the house grows too talkative for you, the little bell at the corner of the screen remembers your silence.

22 OCTOBER 2026

The house learns to move.

The front door is alive today: a ribbon of house lines under the hero, a clock that keeps India time beside the twelve-rung ladder, and three questions of the Golden Knock-off that anyone may play at the door.

Gold dust drifts behind the headline now. It costs nothing and weighs less than a photograph.

21 OCTOBER 2026

Two new rooms, and a line at the foot of every page.

The house opened a Support room and a Delete-your-account room, so that anyone may ask for help, or ask to be forgotten, without opening the app.

And every page now ends with the same quiet line: this platform is maintained by 'Infinity'.
