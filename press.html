What Ivory is, in plain words.

Ivory is a private, adult-only storytelling membership app for Android: real-life stories, voice notes, film and shared moments, gathered in one considered house. The app is released directly by the house through its official release page; this website is informational and carries no checkout and no payments.

Two sentences you may quote

"Ivory is a private world of storytelling for adults — stories, voice and film, chosen and kept in order by the house itself. It is a membership, not a feed: quiet by design, golden by manner."

Facts, without decoration

  • Platform: Android, installed from the official Ivory release page.
  • Audience: adults of 18 and over. No nudity, no explicit content, no in-person meetings.
  • Languages at the first stage: English, Hindi, Bengali, Hinglish and Banglish, chosen before sign-in.
  • Payments and membership live inside the app only; the website sells nothing.
  • The emblem and the name "Ivory · Golden Edition" may be used to describe the app, with a link back to this site.
  • The house is scored: every touch carries its own synthesised note through a soft reverberation — no audio files, no tracking.

Where a partner may appear

  • A single partner line in this website's footer, in plain type, never animated.
  • Inside the app, and only if the house chooses: a sponsor medallion in the Ticker, coloured like the house medals. Nothing else in the app carries advertising, and nothing ever will inside a private session.
