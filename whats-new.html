What's new in the house

Every build of the Ivory Android app, in plain words. Newest first. This page is the official changelog - if a mirror site claims a newer Ivory than this page knows, it is not ours.

House rule for this page: one entry per published build, written the day the build goes live, never promising what the build does not contain.
24 October 2026

Build 1692

  • The whole wordmark, on every phone. The first screen now opens on cream light with the heart, IVORY and the tagline entire - on Android 13, on older phones, on every screen size. The system no longer clips a single letter of it.
  • The dream house answers in one line. The admin switch that rests or wakes the dream house ticker now writes correctly: one quiet sentence confirms it, no error, and the ribbons of the house never stop.
  • Carried from Build 1685: cream-and-pearl system bars on every screen, and roomier spacing in the live-room dialog.
24 October 2026

Build 1685

  • Cream and pearl, edge to edge. The phone's own top and bottom bars now wear the house colours on every screen, day and night.
  • Room to breathe. The live-room dialog no longer crowds its own lines on small screens.
