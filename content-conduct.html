Content & Conduct

Last updated 9 October 2026
Public operator & contact

Ivory is run by one person from India.

Grievance Officer: Indrani Chakraverty · fansupport.ivory@gmail.com

1. What Ivory is

Stories, voice, film and private conversation, made by one artist. Intimate in tone, adult in audience, and suggestive where it wants to be.

2. What Ivory is not

No nudity. No sexual acts, shown or described in explicit detail. No in-person meetings, arranged or hinted at, ever. No escorting, no soliciting, and nothing that treats a member as a transaction.

These are not temporary limits. They are what the house is.

3. Adults only

Every member confirms they are 18 or older. The app is rated for adults. An account found to belong to someone younger is closed and refunded.

4. How members behave

Be decent. Do not demand what section 2 says does not exist. Do not press for a personal number, an address or a meeting. Do not record. Do not repeat a private conversation outside Ivory.

A member who harasses or threatens is removed immediately and without refund. There is no second warning for that one.

5. How the house behaves

The house will never ask for your password, your card number, your UPI PIN or an OTP. The house will never ask you to pay anywhere outside the app. The house will never propose meeting you.

If a message appears to come from Ivory and does any of those things, it is not from Ivory. Report it through the quiet door or contact the Grievance Officer at fansupport.ivory@gmail.com.

6. What happens in a session

A session is between the two people in it. The house does not record. You may not record. Either side may end a session at any moment, for any reason or none.

7. Reporting

The quiet door is on the privacy, safety and terms page and inside the app wherever something can go wrong. It reaches a person, not a queue, and the serious things are dealt with the same day.

8. If something unlawful appears

Tell the house. It is removed on sight, the account behind it is closed, and where the law requires it the matter is reported to the authorities.

9. AI and authenticity

Everything in Ivory is made by the artist. Nothing here is a synthetic likeness of another person, and nothing is presented as someone it is not.
