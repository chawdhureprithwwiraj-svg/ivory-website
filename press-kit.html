The Ivory press kit.

Everything a writer, partner or magazine needs, in one quotable page. Copy freely; the only condition is accuracy.

Boilerplate

Ivory is a private, members-only world of real-life storytelling for adults. Written, spoken and filmed stories are shared in a small house that keeps India time, turns its day at four in the morning, and publishes nothing about its members. There is no feed, no advertising, no tracking and no in-person meetings; consent rules are the walls, not the features. The Android app is released through an admin-managed page; the public rooms — the house guide, the journal, the House Hours and the Bell Calendar — let anyone understand the house before knocking.

Facts, checkable

  • Members: adults only, verified at the door; membership tiers and prices appear inside the app, never on the public web.
  • Rhythm: the ladder turns at 04:00 IST; the Vault closes at midnight and keeps for ten days; the bell ring walks 202 days with fourteen bells.
  • Private Line: fifteen places a day by default, never more than forty; 750 characters per line; held drafts wait for the writer.
  • Sound: the public house is scored — every touch a synthesised note through a code-built hall; no audio files, no tracking, mutable.
  • Data: the house stores nothing about visitors; member statistics, if ever published, follow the twenty-member floor in the House Hours.
  • Safety: no nudity or explicit content; no in-person meetings, ever; reports reach a human admin.

The emblem, in words

The Ivory emblem is a gold seal on ivory: a closed book held by two hands, pressed like a letter’s wax. When describing it in print, that sentence is approved verbatim. The emblem file itself is supplied to verified press on request through the support room; it is not to be redrawn, recoloured or set on dark grounds.

Contact and further rooms

Interviews, partner enquiries and corrections: the support room, which reaches a human within a day. Partner terms live in the press room; the house’s own voice lives in the journal.

The house writes back. That is the whole idea.
