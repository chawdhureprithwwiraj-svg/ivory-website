A promise, not a footnote

Intimate in tone.
Clear about its boundaries.

Ivory is for adults. It does not contain nudity or explicit sexual content, and it does not arrange or suggest in-person meetings. Private sessions are not recorded by the house. The full Terms, Privacy, Refunds and Content & Conduct pages are available below.

A few clear answers

Before you step inside.

Who is Ivory for?

Ivory is for adults aged 18 and over, with stories, voice, film and private membership experiences.

What kind of content is shared?

Stories, voice notes, films, images and polls. Ivory does not contain nudity or explicit sexual content.

Are private sessions recorded?

The house does not record sessions. The Terms of Use prohibit members from recording or sharing private sessions.

Does Ivory arrange in-person meetings?

No. Ivory does not arrange or suggest in-person meetings.

Can I purchase a membership on this website?

No. This is informational only; there is no account sign-in or checkout. Review any applicable terms and prices inside the app before choosing a paid feature.
