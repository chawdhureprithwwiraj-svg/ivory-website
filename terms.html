Ivory is a private place for stories, voice, film and conversation made by one artist for the people who choose to be here. There is no nudity and no explicit content. There are no meetings in person, ever, and nobody from the house will suggest one.

Your account is yours alone. Do not share the login. If you think someone else has reached it, tell the house immediately through the quiet door and it will be locked while it is sorted out.

If you cannot make a session, move it. The reschedule button is there for exactly that and there is no charge for using it.
