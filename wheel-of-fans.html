The Fortune Purse

Every rupee the feather drops lands in your Fortune Purse. The cycle closes one month after your first spin; whatever remains blooms into laurel petals owed to you — nothing is ever confiscated. The purse spends whole on any membership, session, gift or single post whose price is greater than it, and the house rule stands: one instrument per bill.

Gift-a-Spin

Mint a waiting gift and send its code to a friend with the house’s own share hand. Whoever carries the code home gains a Gilded Spin — and so do you, when they claim it. One waiting gift per member per thirty days; a code opens once, and never for its own minter. Kindness circulates; the wheel recruits without a single advertisement.
